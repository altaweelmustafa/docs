## Chapter 3 goal

Before modern block ciphers (DES, AES — later chapters), it's worth understanding the classical techniques they evolved from, and how attackers break them. The chapter closes with a different idea entirely: hiding the *existence* of a message rather than its meaning.

Study order:

1. Basic terminology and the symmetric cipher model.
2. Substitution techniques: Caesar, monoalphabetic, Playfair, Hill.
3. Polyalphabetic ciphers: Vigenère, the Vernam cipher, and the one-time pad.
4. Transposition techniques: rail fence, row transposition.
5. Steganography and digital watermarking.
