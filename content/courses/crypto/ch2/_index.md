## Chapter 2 goal

Number theory shows up everywhere in cryptography, especially asymmetric (public-key) algorithms. This chapter covers the minimum toolkit you need before those chapters make sense.

Study order:

1. Divisibility and the division algorithm.
2. The Euclidean algorithm and the greatest common divisor (GCD).
3. Modular arithmetic and its properties.
4. Prime numbers, and Fermat's and Euler's theorems.
