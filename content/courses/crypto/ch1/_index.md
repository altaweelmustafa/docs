## Chapter 1 goal

This chapter sets the vocabulary for the whole course: what security means, how attacks are classified, what services defend against them, and how cryptographic algorithms are grouped.

Study order:

1. Cybersecurity, information security, and network security — how the terms relate.
2. Security objectives: confidentiality, integrity, availability, and the extended CIA model.
3. The OSI security architecture: attacks, mechanisms, services.
4. Security attacks: passive vs. active.
5. Security services and mechanisms.
6. The three families of cryptographic algorithms: keyless, single-key, two-key.
