## Chapter 1 goal

20% of the exam. This is the "speak the language" chapter: layers, addressing, cabling, and switching basics that everything else builds on.

Study order:

1. OSI/TCP-IP model, TCP vs UDP, devices, cabling.
2. IPv4 addressing and subnetting (this is the part you must be fast at).
3. IPv6 addressing.
4. Switching concepts and virtualization.
5. Hands-on drills.
