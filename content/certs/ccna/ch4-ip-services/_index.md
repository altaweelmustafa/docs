## Chapter 4 goal

10% of the exam. A grab bag of services every network runs. Mostly memorization: port numbers and what each protocol does.

Study order:

1. NAT (static, dynamic, PAT).
2. DHCP, DNS, NTP, SNMP, syslog.
3. QoS basics and remote management (SSH, TFTP/FTP).
4. Hands-on drills.
